## 3. Scope of Software (Shoulder-Surfing Deterrence)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Visual Deterrent vs. Cryptographic Security Disclaimer**:  
> Confitext is designed exclusively as an in-editor visual privacy shield and shoulder-surfing deterrent. It obfuscates character presentation on your physical screen during typing, reading, and presentations.
> 
> Confitext **DOES NOT** provide cryptographic data encryption at rest or in transit:
> - It does not encrypt your `.md` files on disk.
> - Anyone with physical access to your filesystem, cloud storage, Git history, or Obsidian developer tools console can read your notes.
> - If you require cryptographic security for sensitive documents, use specialized full-disk encryption (BitLocker, FileVault, LUKS) or encrypted storage containers.
