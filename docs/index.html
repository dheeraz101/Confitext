Non-Destructive Layer

CodeMirror 6 Replacement Architecture

Confitext never alters characters inside your Markdown documents. Visual masking exists entirely as in-memory CodeMirror 6 StateField decorations. Selecting or copying text extracts clean, unmodified Markdown. Your vault remains 100% standard plain text.

Cross-Platform Ergonomics

Tailored for your keyboard.
Calibrated for your touch.

Workstation power users and mobile note-takers have fundamentally different physical needs. Confitext adapts natively to each platform's hardware ergonomics.

🖥️
macOS & Windows PC Pro
Workstation Precision
  • Hold Esc to Peek: Press and hold the physical Escape key for an instant momentary glance; release Esc to immediately restore the visual veil.
  • Multi-Monitor Window Blur: Automatically engages Matrix veil when the cursor exits the Obsidian window boundaries onto another monitor.
  • Configurable Hover Glance: 200ms to 1200ms precision peek delay calibrated for high-DPI mouse sensors.
  • SF Mono & OpenType Ligatures: Native support for zero-width monospace kerning across JetBrains Mono, Cascadia Code, and SF Mono.
📱
iOS & Android Touch Shield
Touch & Curved Display Comfort
  • 24px–26px Circular Corner Insets: Generous inner card padding prevents text, switches, textareas, and buttons from colliding with native Android curved card corners.
  • Zero-Latency Cold Startup Veil: Instant blur protection completely eliminates the plaintext exposure gap on Android phone cold launch.
  • Subtle Haptic Ticks: Tactile feedback confirmation on mode transitions, word reveals, and emergency panic conceal.
  • Battery-Preserving 30 FPS Mode: Smart frame throttling protects mobile battery life and prevents device thermal throttling.
