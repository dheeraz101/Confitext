HIGH-INTEGRITY ARCHITECTURE

Designed for Uncompromising Privacy

Confitext operates strictly inside CodeMirror 6's screen presentation layer. Clean, robust, and zero-compromise.

🛡️

100% Local & Zero Telemetry

Zero outbound network connections, zero analytics trackers, and zero telemetry. All masking runs strictly in-memory inside your local Obsidian vault.

📝

Non-Destructive In-Memory Layer

Your notes are never rewritten or encrypted on disk. Copying or selecting text always yields standard, readable Markdown.

🎯

Targeted Words & Sensitive Shield

Mask all words or selectively conceal sensitive terms, API keys, passwords, credit cards, emails, and custom regular expressions.

⚡

Granular Hide Triggers

Choose your privacy rhythm: Word mode (scrambles on space/punctuation), Line mode (keeps active line visible), or Paragraph mode.

👁️

Fluid In-Place Reveals

Highlight text with your cursor or keyboard arrow keys, or hover over any word for a quick glance to inspect in-place instantly.

⚡

Instant Panic Conceal

Hit the emergency panic shortcut to deselect your cursor and instantly mask your entire viewport in a fraction of a millisecond.

DESKTOP & MOBILE TAILORED

Engineered Specifically for Every Device

From multi-monitor desktop workstations to curved-screen mobile smartphones, Confitext adapts seamlessly.

🖥️
macOS Sequoia & PC Desktop Pro
Workstation Performance
  • Multi-Monitor Edge Blur: Automatically engages Matrix veil when the cursor exits the Obsidian window onto another display.
  • Configurable Hover Glance: 200ms to 1200ms precision peek delay calibrated for high-DPI mouse sensors.
  • SF Mono & OpenType Ligatures: Native support for zero-width monospace kerning across JetBrains Mono, Cascadia Code, and SF Mono.
  • Native Keyboard Shortcuts: One-touch hotkey toggles for masking mode and emergency panic hide.
📱
iOS & Android Mobile Shield
Touch & Curved Screen Comfort
  • Circular Corner Radius Comfort: Generous 24px-26px horizontal inset padding ensures text, buttons, and switches never touch curved phone display corners.
  • Zero-Latency Cold Startup: Synchronous fast-cache eliminates cleartext exposure gaps when opening Obsidian on Android phones in public.
  • Subtle Haptic Ticks: Tactile feedback confirmation on mode transitions, word reveals, and emergency panic conceal.
  • Battery-Preserving 30 FPS Mode: Smart frame throttling protects mobile battery life and prevents device thermal throttling.
