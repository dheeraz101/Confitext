LEGAL TRANSPARENCY MANIFESTO

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Version: 2.6.0 • Initiative: An YABP Initiative

Core Privacy Guarantee: Confitext does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or analyze your personal information, notes, device identifiers, or usage telemetry. All software execution is 100% client-side and completely offline.

1. Zero Telemetry & Zero Analytics

At Confitext, privacy is the foundational reason the software exists. Unlike software that bundles diagnostic pings, analytics SDKs, or background telemetry, Confitext is completely tracker-free:

  • No Usage Telemetry: We do not track when you open Obsidian, what commands you execute, or how long you type.
  • No User Identification: Confitext has no user registration, no login system, and no account identifiers.
  • No Error Reporting to External Servers: Exceptions or debugging statements remain strictly in your local browser developer console.

2. Complete Absence of Network Activity

Confitext contains zero network communication code. Specifically:

  • There are no calls to fetch(), XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, or third-party cloud APIs.
  • No external stylesheets, fonts, web components, or tracking pixels are fetched from remote Content Delivery Networks (CDNs). All glyphs and styles are self-contained within the plugin package.
  • The plugin works identically in air-gapped, offline, and airplane-mode environments.

3. Local Vault Storage Exclusivity

All data created or configured through Confitext remains strictly confined to your local Obsidian vault:

  • Plugin Settings: Stored locally in <YourVault>/.obsidian/plugins/confitext/data.json.
  • Fast Startup Cache: Stored in your local client's localStorage to ensure 0ms cold boot masking without plaintext flashes.
  • Targeted Note Words: Stored directly inside your local note's YAML frontmatter (confitext-masked-words: [...]).

4. Non-Destructive In-Memory Visual Layer

Confitext does not modify, encrypt, rewrite, or hash your underlying Markdown files (.md). Masking is applied in-memory through CodeMirror 6's Decoration.replace presentation layer:

  • Selecting or copying text extracts clean, readable Markdown without visual glyph corruption.
  • Disabling or uninstalling Confitext leaves your Markdown notes 100% intact and immediately readable in any standard text editor.

5. Regulatory Standards (GDPR, CCPA & Data Protection)

Because Confitext processes zero personal data and maintains zero servers, it exceeds the requirements of major international data protection frameworks:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation): Confitext embodies Data Protection by Design and by Default (Article 25). No personal data is stored or processed on external servers.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act): Confitext does not sell, share, or retain consumer personal information.
  • HIPAA & Workplace Privacy: Confitext aids compliance by preventing unauthorized visual observation (shoulder surfing) of confidential records on public screens.

6. Open Source Verifiability

Confitext is licensed under the permissive MIT License. Every line of TypeScript source code, build scripts, and dependencies is public, open, and auditable on GitHub:

Official Repository: https://github.com/dheeraz101/Confitext
