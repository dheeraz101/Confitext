3. Critical Security Disclaimer: Shoulder-Surfing Deterrence vs. Cryptographic Encryption

IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTICE: Confitext is designed exclusively as an in-editor visual privacy shield and shoulder-surfing deterrent. It obfuscates character presentation on physical displays during typing, reading, and presentations.

Confitext DOES NOT provide cryptographic data encryption at rest or in transit:

  • It does not encrypt or hash your .md files on local storage or solid-state drives.
  • Anyone with direct filesystem access, access to your cloud backups (e.g., Dropbox, Google Drive), Git repository history, or Obsidian developer tools console can view your notes in standard cleartext.
  • If you require cryptographic protection for highly sensitive records, you must employ full-disk hardware encryption (e.g., Apple FileVault, Windows BitLocker, Linux LUKS) or encrypted storage containers (e.g., Cryptomator, VeraCrypt).
